Our Story

The name Resonancia, the Spanish word for resonance, speaks to the lasting relationship between architecture, people, culture, and place. We believe that a well-designed space should create an experience that extends beyond its walls, one that can be felt, remembered, and carried forward.

Atelier, meaning studio or workshop, reflects our approach to design: thoughtful, collaborative, and crafted with intention. Every project is treated as an opportunity to translate ideas, stories, and aspirations into spaces with character and purpose.

Our work brings together architecture, artistry, context, and human experience, seeking a balance between beauty and function, permanence and adaptability, identity and innovation.

We design not simply for today, but for the moments that will happen within our spaces, and for the memories that will remain long after.

Because architecture should not merely be seen.
It should be experienced.
It should be remembered.
It should resonate.”

Ar. Mary Rose

Founder & Principal Architect

Mary Rose Samson founded Resonancia Atelier in 2023 with a vision shaped by both architecture and art.

A graduate of Bachelor of Science in Architecture from Holy Angel University, Mary Rose is also a Registered Master Plumber, having passed the Master Plumber Licensure Examination in July 2024. Her multidisciplinary background allows her to approach design with both creative sensitivity and technical depth.

Her vision for Resonancia Atelier was inspired by a friend who encouraged her to dream beyond simply becoming an architect,to build something of her own and pursue the lifelong ambition of becoming a master designer.

Resonancia Atelier is the expression of that pursuit: an atelier where architecture, artistry, and technical mastery converge, created with intention, refined with discipline, and imagined to endure.

OUR MISSION

We craft architecture that listens before it speaks. Rooted in context, guided by intention, and elevated by artistry, our work harmonizes structure and soul.

We believe buildings should not dominate their environment, they should resonate with it.

OUR VISION

To become a globally respected Filipino-led design atelier known for architecture that balances elegance, sustainability, and cultural narrative, shaping spaces that endure both structurally and emotionally.

Let's create something that resonates.

Every project starts with a conversation.
