Baño

Baño is a modern bathroom interior design that emphasizes simplicity, elegance, and comfort. Inspired by natural materials and neutral colors, the space creates a calm and relaxing atmosphere. Its combination of beige stone finishes, warm metallic fixtures, soft lighting, and minimalist details results in a timeless design that balances functionality with visual appeal.
