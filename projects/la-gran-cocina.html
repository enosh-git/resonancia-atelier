La Gran Cocina

La Gran Cocina is a modern luxury kitchen inspired by Timeless Hospitality, combining marble, Patagonia stone, and warm brass accents to create an elegant yet inviting atmosphere. Concealed storage maintains a clean, refined aesthetic, while a sculptural chandelier becomes the focal point. The design maximizes functionality and spatial efficiency within a compact 24 sqm footprint.
