Paran House

Paran House is a contemporary residential design that showcases modern luxury through bold geometric forms, contrasting materials, and a strong connection with nature. The house features a multi-level composition, expansive windows, cantilevered architectural elements, and a striking combination of white and charcoal finishes. Surrounded by mature trees and lush greenery, the residence creates a balance between privacy, sophistication, and openness. Its distinctive waterfall feature further enhances the architectural experience, transforming the exterior into a visually engaging and tranquil living environment.
